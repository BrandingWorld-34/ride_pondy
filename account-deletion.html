Account Deletion Policy

Effective Date: 10 November 2026
Application Name: Ride Pondy

1. Account Deletion

Ride Pondy users can request the deletion of their registered accounts and associated personal information at any time.

We respect your privacy and provide an option to request account deletion if you no longer wish to use our services.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

To request the deletion of your Ride Pondy account, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Ride Pondy mobile application.
  2. Navigate to your Profile or Account Settings.
  3. Select the Account Deletion option, if available.
  4. Confirm your account deletion request.

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by sending an email to:

Email: ridepondy@gmail.com

Subject: Ride Pondy – Account Deletion Request

Please include your registered mobile number and account details to help us verify your identity.

3. Information Deleted

Once your account deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete or anonymize the personal information associated with your account, including:

  • Name and profile information.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Email address, if provided.
  • Login and account information.
  • Personal account-related information.

4. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when necessary for:

  • Legal and regulatory compliance.
  • Tax and accounting requirements.
  • Resolving pending bookings or payment disputes.
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes.
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations.

Such information will be retained only for the period required by applicable laws and legitimate business requirements.

5. Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days.

If additional time is required due to legal, technical, or operational reasons, we will inform the user accordingly.

6. Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Once your account is deleted, you may lose access to your booking history, profile information, and other account-related services.

If you wish to use Ride Pondy again, you may need to create a new account.
